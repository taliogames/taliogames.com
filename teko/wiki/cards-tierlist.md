<h2 style="text-align: center;">TEKO u4.0 - Best CARDS for beginners!</h2>

<p align="center">
  <img src="/assets/tierlist1.jpg" style="max-width: 100%; height: auto; display: block; margin: 0 auto;">
</p>
The criterion used to measure whether a card is easy for a beginner to use is how easy it's to include within a build. Note that the simplest cards usually have "flat" effects, while the more complex ones involve more pocket management and learning how to optimize interest.

For example, cards such as TRIXI, TEKANO, Another Chance, and Trickster provide immediate advantages without any kind of drawback. This does not mean they are the most powerful cards in TEKO, but they tend to be more versatile and don't require scaling, making them the easiest to use, especially for someone who is just starting to play.

Other cards, such as Tryhard, Near the Edge, Gold Digger, Hammer, The Beast, and Fair Deal, require a higher level of risk, learning how to manage combos, draw count, and other elements. At the same time, learning how to maximize these cards can turn them into major advantages. Difficult does not mean weak.

You should also keep in mind that some cards are categorized as “EASY” simply because they are easy to use, but they don't reach the level of immediacy of those in the highest tier. The same applies to "MEDIUM" cards, which have many ways to scale and therefore can be managed more easily than "HARD" ones.

Remember: difficulty is 100% subjective and depends on each player. Players who are used to forcing a specific type of build may disagree with some placements in the tier list, because they will feel that their favorite cards are stronger and easier to use than the others.
