# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** [To be completed]

This Privacy Policy explains how **Talio Games** ("we," "us," or "our") collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you play **TEKO** or interact with our official services.

By using TEKO, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Data We Collect

Talio Games collects only the information necessary to analyze gameplay and improve the balance of TEKO.

Gameplay data may include, but is not limited to:

* Steam ID.
* Steam display name.
* Game version.
* Difficulty level.
* Cards used.
* Chips collected.
* Gameplay choices.
* Match outcomes.
* Other gameplay-related information generated during a play session.

Talio Games does **not** intentionally collect personal information such as your real name, postal address, phone number, payment information, or similar personal identifiers through the Game.

## 2. Purpose of Data Collection

The gameplay data collected is used exclusively for improving TEKO.

This includes purposes such as:

* Balancing gameplay mechanics.
* Identifying underused or overpowered content.
* Detecting balancing issues.
* Improving future updates.
* General game design analysis.

Gameplay data is **not** used for advertising, profiling, marketing, or automated decision-making.

## 3. How Data Is Collected

Gameplay statistics are transmitted securely from the Game to a Google Sheets document through Google Apps Script.

Talio Games does not operate dedicated servers for gameplay data collection.

## 4. Steam Services

TEKO requires a Steam account to play.

The Game also supports Steam Cloud, which allows players to synchronize save files between devices.

Steam Cloud is operated entirely by Valve Corporation and is governed by Steam's own Privacy Policy.

Players may use TEKO completely offline. While offline, Steam Cloud synchronization will simply not occur until Steam reconnects.

## 5. Data Sharing

Talio Games does not sell, rent, trade, or otherwise share your personal information with third parties.

Gameplay information collected by Talio Games is used solely for internal development purposes.

## 6. Data Retention

Gameplay statistics are retained only while they remain useful for analyzing the current version of TEKO.

When a major update or new version is released, previously collected gameplay statistics may be permanently deleted if they are no longer required.

Important support or legal correspondence received through email may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary.

## 7. Data Security

Talio Games implements reasonable measures to protect collected information against unauthorized access.

Access to gameplay data is restricted to Talio Games only.

Although reasonable security measures are used, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

## 8. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

* Request access to the information Talio Games holds about you.
* Request correction of inaccurate information.
* Request deletion of your personal information where applicable.
* Object to or restrict certain processing activities where permitted by law.

Requests may be submitted using the contact information provided below.

## 9. Children's Privacy

TEKO is intended for a general audience.

Talio Games does not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond the gameplay information necessary for operating and improving the Game.

If a parent or legal guardian believes that information has been collected inappropriately, they may contact us to request its review or deletion where applicable.

## 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Talio Games may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.

When significant changes are made, we will make reasonable efforts to notify players through our official communication channels.

The latest version will always replace any previous version.
